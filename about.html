Who we are

Unsurpassed craftsmanship

At US Food Truck Factory we specialize in custom fabrication to meet all our client’s needs. We convert step vans, box trucks, buses and trailers into fully functional mobile kitchens.

The work is done in-house by skilled fabricators, electricians, technicians and installers—so the people who design your layout are the same people who weld, wire and plumb it.

Advanced machinery gives every build a clean, flawless and professional finish, and our leadership in graphic design means your logo, menu boards and truck wrap all come from one place.

How we build

Setting a higher standard within the standard of commercial kitchens

A mobile kitchen has to pass inspection, survive highway vibration and still be a good place to work a twelve-hour shift. We build for all three.

  • NSF-rated stainless surfaces throughout the service area
  • Three-compartment sink and separate hand wash on every food build
  • Hood systems with fire suppression over the cook line
  • Electrical, propane and plumbing installed to current code
  • Every unit inspected and tested before it leaves the shop
