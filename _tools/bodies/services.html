Common questions

How long does a custom build take?

Timelines depend on the vehicle, the equipment list and how quickly parts arrive. A straightforward trailer build moves faster than a full step-van conversion. We give you a realistic schedule with your quote and keep you updated through every stage.

Do you supply the vehicle, or can I bring my own?

Both. We can source a suitable step van, box truck, bus or trailer for you, or build out a vehicle you already own. If you are shopping on your own, talk to us first—we will tell you what to look for and what to avoid.

Will my truck pass health and fire inspection?

Every build is fabricated to meet commercial kitchen code: NSF-rated surfaces, three-compartment sink with hand wash, proper ventilation, and a hood with fire suppression where required. Requirements vary by county and state, so tell us where you will operate and we will build to that jurisdiction.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Our financing process is simple, quick and easy, with competitive rates. Most applicants get an answer in 24 hours or less. Read more about financing.

Do you deliver outside Maryland?

We do. Our builds are on the road in all 50 states. Delivery is arranged as part of your quote.
